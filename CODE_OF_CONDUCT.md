# Code of conduct

Skwod is a small project made by friends and for friends. We want taking part in it to feel the
same way: welcoming, honest and safe for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion
or sexual orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good faith. Most friction is a misunderstanding.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people. Explain the "why" behind a review comment.
- Accept a "no" gracefully, and give one with a reason.
- Respect people's time and privacy. This is a privacy project, so never post someone's personal
  details, messages, or anything from their Skwod without their consent.
- Credit other people's work.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Publishing someone's private information (doxxing), or pressuring them to share it.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, or keeping up behaviour after you've been asked to stop.

## Where it applies

In this repository (issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions) and anywhere you represent the
project, including a Skwod that's run for contributors.

## Reporting

E-mail **hello@skwod.io** with what happened, where, and links or screenshots if you have them.
Reports are read only by the maintainers and kept confidential. If a maintainer is involved, say
so and they won't take part in handling it.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues or other contributions that break this code,
and may warn, or temporarily or permanently ban, anyone who does. We'll tell the person why.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
